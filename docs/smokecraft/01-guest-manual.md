# SmokeCraft Guest Manual

**Version:** MVP2 · **Audience:** Guests and first-time visitors

---

## What is SmokeCraft?

SmokeCraft is a guided cigar education journey offered exclusively at participating NOVEE OS venues. Over 8 visits and 24 sessions, you build knowledge of tobacco origins, cultivation, blending, flavor profiles, and the art of the cigar.

## Your Journey at a Glance

| Visit | Sessions | Theme |
|-------|----------|-------|
| 1 | 1–3 | Introduction & Intake |
| 2 | 4–6 | Leaves & Cultivation |
| 3 | 7–9 | Blend & Flavor DNA |
| 4 | 10–12 | Pairing & Availability |
| 5 | 13–15 | Terroir & Mastery |
| 6 | 16–18 | Vitola & Identity |
| 7 | 19–21 | Advanced Tasting |
| 8 | 22–24 | Final Review & Stamp |

## Starting Your First Session

1. Arrive at a SmokeCraft-enabled venue.
2. Scan the QR code on your table or ask a staff member to open SmokeCraft.
3. Tap **Begin Your Journey** on the SmokeCraft home screen.
4. Complete the intake enrollment form.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts through each session.

## Progress & Locks

- Each visit unlocks when you complete the previous one.
- Sessions within a visit are completed in order.
- Your progress is saved to this device. If you switch devices, ask venue staff for a transfer code.

## Tasting Notes

During the First Third, Second Third, and Final Third sessions, you will be asked to select tasting notes. These choices are recorded to your flavor profile and contribute to your Cigar Identity score.

## Passport Stamps

Completing a full visit earns you a Passport Stamp. Stamps accumulate in your NOVEE Passport and contribute to the venue leaderboard.

## Leaderboard & Challenges

The leaderboard shows top guests by total XP and stamp count. Event challenges may award bonus XP during special venue events.

## Need Help?

Tap **Request Assistance** at any point during your session, and a SmokeCraft-trained staff member will come to your table.
