# SmokeCraft Staff Operations Guide

**Version:** MVP2 · **Audience:** Venue staff (role: `staff`)

---

## Staff Capabilities

Staff may:
- View active guest sessions on the floor
- Assist guests on request (trigger Assist Guest flow)
- Confirm purchases during the Request Purchase session
- View floor assist requests

Staff may NOT:
- Modify guest progress or session data
- Access management sync, inventory, or venue configuration
- Access error logs or audit trails
- Toggle feature flags

## Opening a Guest Session

If a guest cannot scan the QR code themselves:
1. Log in with your staff credentials at the venue tablet.
2. Navigate to **Floor View** → **Guest Assist**.
3. Select the table or guest name.
4. Hand the tablet to the guest to begin their session.

## Handling a Purchase Request

When a guest reaches the Request Purchase session, a notification appears on the staff console:
1. You receive a floor alert with the guest's table and selection.
2. Verify the item with the humidor or stock.
3. Tap **Confirm** to acknowledge the request in the system.
4. Fulfill the order through standard venue POS flow.

The system will show **Order Pending** until you confirm. It will NOT show **Ordered** or **Confirmed** until you tap Confirm.

## Assist Requests

Guests can tap **Request Assistance** at any session screen. This creates a floor request visible in your console:
- Guest name (or table number)
- Current session name
- Time of request

Tap **Acknowledge** to claim the request. Tap **Resolved** when the guest is helped.

## Demo Mode

If a guest or sales visitor asks to see SmokeCraft without making real progress, a manager can activate Demo Mode. In demo mode:
- All session locks are bypassed
- No passport stamps are awarded
- No POS orders are placed

Ask your manager to activate demo mode. Staff cannot enable it independently.
