# SmokeCraft 360 — Guest Manual

**Your step-by-step guide to the guided cigar experience.**

Welcome to SmokeCraft 360. This is not a menu — it is a guided journey through
eight visits designed to help you discover, taste, and understand the cigar that
is right for you. This manual tells you exactly what to do, what to expect, and
what each screen is asking for, in the order you will encounter it.

---

## Before You Begin

You do not need an account. You do not need to sign in.

When you arrive at SmokeCraft, your host will open the experience on the venue
tablet or your personal device. If you are using your own phone, navigate to the
lounge's SmokeCraft URL and tap **Start New SmokeCraft Session**.

Your progress is saved on the device you start with. If you switch devices, your
session will not carry over automatically. Use the same device for your full visit.

---

## Visit 1 — Your Profile, Your Rules, Your Mentor

### Step 1: SmokeCraft Landing

**What you see:** A full-screen image of the SmokeCraft experience. A button at
the bottom reads **Start New SmokeCraft Session →**.

**What to do:** Tap the button. If you have been here before and are continuing a
previous session, tap **Continue Previous Session** instead.

---

### Step 2: Profile Enrollment (Enroll)

**What you see:** A form asking for your preferences before you start. This may
include your experience level (first-timer, occasional, regular), flavor
preferences, and what you are looking for today.

**What to do:** Answer each question honestly. There are no wrong answers — this
shapes your recommendations throughout the session. When done, tap **Continue →**
at the bottom.

*If you do not want to share details, you can tap Continue without selecting
anything and the system will use general defaults.*

---

### Step 3: Golden Box Rules

**What you see:** The Golden Box rules screen. This explains the Golden Box
system — a special recognition for guests who complete the full tasting journey
and meet the scoring criteria.

**What to do:** Read through the rules. You do not need to opt in — understanding
the rules is all that is required here. Tap **Continue →** when ready.

*What is the Golden Box?* It is the highest reward category in SmokeCraft. You
earn it by completing all tasting steps and achieving a high enough score on the
Scorecard at the end. Your host can tell you more.

---

### Step 4: Mentor Selection

**What you see:** A grid of mentors, each representing a tobacco-growing tradition
from a specific country (Dominican Republic, Nicaragua, Honduras, Mexico, Cuba,
Peru, USA, Brazil). Each mentor has a name, a flag, and a specialty.

**What to do:** Tap the mentor whose tradition sounds most interesting to you. You
can choose up to two mentors. Your mentors will influence the cigar and pairing
recommendations you receive throughout the session.

*You can change your mentors on your next visit.*

---

## Visit 2 — Cigar Education

### Step 5: Shape, Size & Burn Time (Format)

**What you see:** Images and descriptions of different cigar shapes (Robusto,
Churchill, Torpedo, Perfecto, Panatela, and more) with notes on burn time and
draw intensity.

**What to do:** Browse the formats. You will be asked to select the format that
appeals to you or that your host recommends. Tap your choice, then tap
**Continue →**.

---

### Step 6: Wrapper & Strength (Wrapper Strength)

**What you see:** A guide to cigar wrapper colors and their relationship to
strength — from Claro (light) through Colorado, Colorado Maduro, Maduro, and
Oscuro (full). The screen also covers body and strength differences.

**What to do:** Read the descriptions. Select the strength profile that matches
what you want today. If you are unsure, your host can advise. Tap **Continue →**.

---

## Visit 3 — Origin & Pairing Intelligence

### Step 7: Seed & Soil Pairing

**What you see:** A visual guide to tobacco-growing regions and how the soil and
climate affect the flavor of the leaf. You may also see a pairing recommendation
based on your mentor selection and preferences.

**What to do:** Review the pairing. If the system shows a recommended cigar style
or origin, this is based on what you told us in your profile. Tap **Continue →**.

---

## Visit 4 — Selection & Preparation

### Step 8: Pairing Lab

**What you see:** A deeper look at how your cigar pairs with a drink or food item.
This is the pairing intelligence screen — you may see a spirit, a beverage, or a
culinary pairing recommendation.

**What to do:** Review the pairing suggestion. If you want to try a different
pairing, you can explore options shown on screen. Tap **Continue →** when ready.

---

### Step 9: Humidor Match

**What you see:** A recommendation from the humidor based on your profile,
mentor, format, and pairing choices. The screen shows one or more cigars from the
venue's available selection.

**What to do:** Review the recommended cigar. If you would like a different
recommendation, let your host know. Tap **Continue →** to proceed to purchase.

---

### Step 10: Request / Purchase

**What you see:** A screen where you can formally request the cigar selected for
you. This sends a notification to the floor staff.

**What to do:** Tap **Request this Cigar** or follow the prompt on screen. Your
host will bring the cigar to you. Wait for your host before continuing.

*Note: This is a request to staff — it is not a payment screen. Your bill will
be settled through the venue's standard process.*

---

### Step 11: Cut, Toast & Light

**What you see:** A guide to the three preparation methods: cutting (Straight Cut,
V-Cut, Punch Cut), toasting (Gentle Toast, Foot Toast, Full Toast), and lighting
(Cedar Spill, Torch Lighter, Soft Flame). You can select your preferred method
for each.

**What to do:** Choose your cutting method, toasting technique, and lighting
method by tapping each chip. Your selections are recorded but do not lock you in
— your host will assist with the actual preparation. Tap **Continue to First
Third →** when ready.

*If you are unsure which methods to choose, your host will recommend based on
your cigar.*

---

## Visit 4 (continued) — The Tasting

### Step 12: First Third (First Third)

**What you see:** Tasting notes and sensory cues for the first third of your
cigar. This is typically the cooler, less concentrated section. The screen may
show flavors to look for: earth, cedar, leather, creaminess.

**What to do:** Light your cigar (your host will assist). Smoke the first third.
Record your impressions by tapping the flavors or descriptors you notice. The
screen may include sliders for draw quality and smoke production.

Take your time — there is no rush. Tap **Continue to Second Third →** only when
you have finished the first third.

---

### Step 13: Second Third

**What you see:** Tasting notes and sensory cues for the second third. This is
typically where the cigar hits its peak complexity. Flavors often become richer
and more defined.

**What to do:** Continue smoking. Record your impressions. Note any changes from
the first third — new flavors, increased strength, changes in draw. Tap
**Continue to Final Third →** when ready.

---

### Step 14: Flavor Memory

**What you see:** A short sensory capture screen. You may be asked to lock in
your most prominent flavor memory from the first two thirds before moving to the
final assessment.

**What to do:** Select the one or two flavors that stood out most to you so far.
Tap **Continue →**.

---

## Visit 6 — Final Tasting & Scorecard

### Step 15: Final Third

**What you see:** The final tasting section. This is where the cigar is at its
most concentrated and often most complex. Finish your cigar while noting the
experience.

**What to do:** Record your final impressions. The screen may ask you to rate the
overall experience or flag any issues (harshness, burn problems, draw issues).
Tap **Continue to Scorecard →** when done.

---

### Step 16: Scorecard

**What you see:** Your full tasting scorecard. This compiles your draw quality,
flavor notes, aroma impressions, finish length, complexity, and overall
satisfaction into a structured score.

**What to do:** Review your score. You can adjust any individual rating before
submitting. When satisfied, tap **Submit Scorecard →**. Your score is recorded
for this session.

*What happens with your score?* It is compared against the Golden Box criteria,
recorded in your Passport, and used to generate your session ranking if you
choose to enter the SmokeCraft Challenge.

---

### Step 17: SmokeCraft Challenge

**What you see:** Information about the current SmokeCraft Challenge at this
venue. Challenges may reward the highest score, the most consistent taster, or
the best matching of flavor to region.

**What to do:** Review the challenge details. You can enter the challenge by
tapping **Enter Challenge** or skip it by tapping **Continue →**.

---

### Step 18: Second Humidor Match

**What you see:** A follow-up humidor recommendation. Now that your scorecard is
complete, the system uses your full tasting history to suggest your next cigar
for a future visit.

**What to do:** Review the recommendation. No action is required — this is a
forward-looking suggestion. Tap **Continue →**.

---

### Step 19: Mini Tasting Round

**What you see:** A short sensory check focused on one or two specific flavor
categories. This is a bonus evaluation round, not a full tasting.

**What to do:** Complete the mini round — it takes about two minutes. Tap
**Continue →**.

---

## Visit 7 — Your Passport

### Step 20: Final Review

**What you see:** A summary of your entire session — your profile, your mentor,
your cigar, your tasting notes, your scores, and your journey milestones.

**What to do:** Review your session summary. This is your record of the visit.
Tap **Continue to Passport Stamp →** when ready.

---

### Step 21: Passport Stamp

**What you see:** Your SmokeCraft 360 Passport receiving a new stamp. Each visit
earns a different stamp. Your collection builds over time.

**What to do:** Tap **Claim Your Stamp →** to officially record this visit.
Your stamp will appear in your Passport on future visits.

---

### Step 22: Connections

**What you see:** Your Passport Connections — other SmokeCraft guests you have
been matched with based on similar preferences, mentors, or flavor profiles.
Connections are anonymous (first name only, unless both guests share contact info).

**What to do:** View your connections. If the venue has enabled the Connections
feature, you may see notes from previous connections. Tap **Continue →**.

---

## Visit 8 — Session Close

### Step 23: Management Sync

**What you see:** A brief summary sent to the venue's management and floor staff.
This includes your purchase, your tasting result, and your experience tier. You
will not see personal data from other guests here — only your session summary.

**What to do:** This screen closes automatically. No action is required from you.

---

### Step 24: Session Complete

**What you see:** Your session is complete. A summary screen shows your final
score, your Golden Box eligibility status, and the stamps you earned today.

**What to do:** Your session is finished. Show the screen to your host if they
need to confirm your session. Your Passport is now updated.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I come back and continue where I left off?**
Yes. On your next visit, tap **Continue Previous Session** on the landing screen.
Your progress is saved on the device. If you return to the same venue with the
same device, your session history will be there.

**I tapped the wrong thing. Can I go back?**
Most screens let you navigate back using the arrow button at the top left. On
screens that have already been completed and saved (like the Scorecard), you may
not be able to change your answers — ask your host.

**What is the Passport?**
Your Passport is your personal record at this lounge. Each visit adds a stamp.
Over time, stamps unlock benefits and recognition tiers at participating venues.

**What is the Golden Box?**
The Golden Box is the highest recognition in the SmokeCraft system. It is awarded
when your final score meets the venue's Golden Box criteria. Not every visit will
qualify — it depends on your cigar, your tasting performance, and the venue's
current Golden Box rules.

**Is my data shared?**
Your tasting data is stored on this device during your session. If the venue has
a backend database connected, your session may be stored there as well. Ask your
host if you have questions about data retention at this venue.

**Something went wrong — the screen is frozen.**
Pull to refresh the page, or ask your host to reload SmokeCraft on the tablet.
Your session progress should be recovered from the last saved state.
