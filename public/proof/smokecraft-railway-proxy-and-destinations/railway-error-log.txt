=== USER-PROVIDED PRODUCTION EVIDENCE (Railway) ===
This excerpt was supplied by the repo owner from the live Railway deployment
logs. It is NOT captured by this pass — it is the confirmed root-cause evidence
that motivated the fix. Reproduced verbatim as provided:

  ERR_ERL_UNEXPECTED_X_FORWARDED_FOR

Context (per user report): Railway operates behind a reverse proxy and sends the
`X-Forwarded-For` header, but Express `trust proxy` was not configured, so
express-rate-limit could not correctly identify the client and threw a validation
error on every request behind that proxy. This is why real production users could
not start a SmokeCraft journey (Start stayed on the Landing page, activeJourneyId
was never created/persisted, protected routes stayed locked) even though local
`vite preview` testing — which has NO reverse proxy in front of it — never
surfaced the failure.
