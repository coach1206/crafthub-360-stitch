## Source of truth

registry — the same one that drives the real route guards, the screen
manifest (`src/constants/smokecraftScreenManifest.js`), and the completion
authority (`server/services/smokecraft/playerStateService.js`). It was not
hand-transcribed for this package; the table below was generated by
importing it directly (see `scripts/smokecraft27SessionAudit.mjs`, already
in the repo from a prior pass, and re-verified live in this pass).

**Total sessions: 27 — MATCH.**
**Total phases: 6** — confirmed directly from source (`VISIT_STRUCTURE.length === 6`, `TOTAL_VISITS === 6`).

> **Documented discrepancy, not silently corrected:** this task's mandate
> says "27 sessions across 7 phases." The repository's one real, locked
> spine has always been 6 phases, not 7. This was already flagged by the
> prior `smokecraft27SessionAudit.mjs` pass and is re-confirmed here by
> direct source read. No phase was merged, split, or renumbered to force a
> match to "7" — that would have been fabricating a fact about the game to
> satisfy a mandate's number instead of reporting the truth.

its own distinct `achievements` id). This is not a defect; it is confirmed
by direct source read of `VISIT_STRUCTURE`'s `mergedInto` / `sharedComponent`
